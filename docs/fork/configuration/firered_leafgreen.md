# FireRed and LeafGreen

Both Kanto versions use the fork's Nuzlite, Nuzlocke, and Custom modes. The
feature menu appears before the controls/Oak introduction. Start a new game;
the expanded save format is incompatible with retail and older fork saves.

## Regional progression

Catch and faint rules activate when Oak gives the Pokédex. Opening battles
remain protected. Kanto caps follow these milestones; starter-dependent rival
variants share a milestone. Existing FRLG boss teams are retained.

When encounter randomization is enabled, Oak's progress check counts all
caught species, so cross-generation Pokémon count toward the National Dex gate.

## Randomizers and rewards

Kanto and Sevii use the same biome pools, generation limit, typing rules, and
save-stable assignments as Emerald. Their map-specific ranges appear in the
[generated reference](../reference/biome_encounter_assignments.csv). Water and
fishing ranges are wider to support distinct species at Generation 3.

Oak's three starter choices are seeded, distinct base-form Pokémon, each with
at least two perfect IVs. Gift and trade outputs are seeded evolving base forms
in the 100–550 BST range when encounter randomization is enabled. Trade inputs
remain the original requested Pokémon. Fossils revive into their corresponding
species; Mt. Moon's choices use the seeded fossil pool and Cinnabar's scientist
accepts all supported fossils. The birds, Mewtwo, Mew, Lugia, and Ho-Oh use the
special static pool. Ordinary static encounters use their location's land pool.

TM moves use the fixed Gen 9 catalog. Gym item rewards are randomized, and
item balls and hidden items use seeded rewards. Progression items and HMs are
protected. Celadon Department Store 2F sells eight seeded TMs, one copy each,
and evolution stones plus the Linking Cord.

## Convenience features

- Viridian Mart's woman provides the early Old Rod.
- Running Shoes and reusable key items are available from the start.
- The PC's **LEVEL BOXES TO CAP** command raises every stored, eligible Pokémon.
- Portable PC is unavailable at Indigo Plateau and Victory Road.
- Field moves require the HM and the appropriate Kanto badge. Rock Smash
  requires its HM without a badge. Flash and Fly appear in non-Egg party menus
  without teaching the move.
- Saffron's Fighting Dojo supplies the regional evolution location used by the
  Trick House in Emerald.

Wild held items, Day Care services, Vs. Seeker rematches, Trainer Tower access,
and the applicable renewable money sources are disabled under the shared
[player rules](../gameplay/player_rules.md). Safari catches, expanded Bag
capacity, EV/faint rules, Mega Evolution, and TM consumption use those same
rules. Hoenn-only activities remain regional; this does not add Hoenn locations
to Kanto.
