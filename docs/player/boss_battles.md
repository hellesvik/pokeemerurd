# Boss Battle Teams

This page lists the main first-clear teams. Levels, abilities, held items,
and moves are shown as configured. If Random Abilities is enabled, eligible
trainer Pokémon can receive the save's randomized ability mapping.

Wattson, Tate & Liza, and the other entries marked **doubles** should be
planned as multi-Pokémon battles, not as six isolated singles.

## Team Aqua and Team Magma leaders

The main late-game leader battles are:

- **Maxie, Magma Hideout:** Mightyena Lv. 37, Crobat Lv. 38, Camerupt Lv. 39.
- **Archie:** Mightyena Lv. 41, Crobat Lv. 41, Mega Sharpedo Lv. 43 holding
  Sharpedonite.
- **Maxie, Mossdeep:** Mightyena Lv. 42, Crobat Lv. 43, Mega Camerupt Lv. 44
  holding Cameruptite.

These leader teams use their configured story abilities and may be affected
by the ability randomizer when that option is enabled.
